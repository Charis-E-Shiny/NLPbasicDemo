# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in CADVoice welcoming and respectful.

Examples of expected behavior include being considerate, communicating constructively, respecting differing viewpoints, and accepting responsibility for mistakes. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately.
